Our Story
Built one small batch at a time.
OBE's makes soap, jewelry, and fragrance by hand — starting as a small side project and growing into an online shop that ships across the Philippines.
How We Started
A small workspace, not a big launch.

OBE's didn't start with a business plan. It started with a handful of soap bars made in small batches, tested with family, and sold to a few interested friends. There was no factory, no funding round — just a workspace, basic equipment, and a willingness to figure things out order by order.

As demand grew past what a spreadsheet could track, we added jewelry and fragrance to the catalog and moved the whole operation online. What changed was the scale. What stayed the same was the process — small batches, checked by hand, before anything ships.
