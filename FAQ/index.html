Support

Questions, answered.

Everything about ordering, delivery, and caring for your OBE's pieces — in one place. Can't find it here? Chat with us in the corner, or reach out directly.

01Ordering & Payment

How do I place an order?
Browse the Shop, add your pieces to cart, and fill in your delivery details at checkout. Once your order is confirmed you'll get a reference number to track it anytime.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept GCash and Cash on Delivery. For GCash, you'll see our account details at checkout — just send payment and confirm via Messenger so we can get your order moving right away.
Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
Message us on Messenger as soon as possible with your order reference. We can usually adjust or cancel an order before it's packed — once it's out for delivery, changes aren't possible.
Is there a minimum order amount?
No minimum to shop with us. Orders that reach our free-delivery threshold get delivery fees waived automatically at checkout.

02Shipping & Delivery

How can I track my order?
Use the Track My Order option with the reference number from your confirmation. You'll see live status from Pending through Packing, On the Way, and Delivered.
How long does delivery take?
Most orders within our service area are delivered within 1–3 days of confirmation, depending on your location and order volume that day.
Do you offer pick-up?
Yes — choose pick-up at checkout and the delivery fee is waived entirely. We'll message you the pick-up details once your order is ready.

03Products & Ingredients

What's in your whitening soap bars?
Each bar is handcrafted with shea butter, oatmeal, and botanical extracts, made in small batches so quality stays consistent from bar to bar.
Is your jewelry hypoallergenic?
Our rings, bracelets, and earrings are made with skin-friendly materials suited for everyday wear. If you have known metal sensitivities, message us before ordering and we'll help you pick the right piece.
How should I store my candles and soap?
Keep candles away from direct sunlight and trim the wick before each use. Soap bars last longest stored on a draining dish, away from standing water between uses.

04Returns & Exchanges

What if my item arrives damaged?
Message us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the item and your order reference. We'll arrange a replacement or refund — whichever works best for you.
Can I exchange a piece for a different size or scent?
Yes, within 7 days of delivery, as long as the item is unused and in its original packaging. Just reach out via Messenger to arrange it.

05Distributor Program

How does the Distributor Program work?
Distributors purchase a product bundle and get a discount on personal reorders plus a commission structure on bundles they sell. Full terms, current pricing, and commission details are reviewed with you directly before you join.
Where can I see the current tiers and pricing?
Tier details and current pricing are on our Contact page — reach out to our team there with any questions before signing up.
Are earnings guaranteed?
No. Any income figures shown are projections based on active selling and network growth — actual results vary by individual effort and are not guaranteed.
